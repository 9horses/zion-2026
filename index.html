Itinerary

Plan as of 2026-11-21. All times Mountain. Daylight about 9 h 40 m; sunset 5:05-5:14 pm everywhere on this loop.

Fixed points

Dec 3-7 Zion Lodge, 4 nights, check-out Mon Dec 7 (11 am, or 1 pm with the winter promo).

Day by day

Date Plan Night
Wed Dec 2 Land 10:01. At the Hertz counter: decline all protection, add Cecilia as driver, check the tires are M+S or 3PMSF. Drive I-15 S, UT-20, US-89 S, UT-12 E to Bryce, 4 h 15; lunch stop at Fillmore or Beaver. Buy the America the Beautiful pass at the Bryce gate ($80, covers all three parks). Arrive about 3:45. Sunset Point for the 5:10 sunset (Bryce map). Dinner Cowboy's Buffet. If UT-20 is closed: I-15 to I-70, I-70 E to US-89, south to Panguitch, 4 h 45. Ruby's Inn, Bryce Canyon City.
Thu Dec 3 Sunrise 7:27 at Sunrise Point (the hoodoos face east; this is the light). Queen's Garden + Navajo Loop via Two Bridges, about 3 mi, microspikes. Inspiration and Bryce Points. Apply for the Dec 4 Angels Landing lottery before leaving (by 3 pm). Leave 12:30, UT-12, US-89 S, UT-9 W through the tunnel, 2 h 15. Canyon Overlook Trail (1 mi) sits just east of the tunnel; do it on the way in if there is light. Check in 4 pm. Dinner 5 pm. Zion Lodge
Fri Dec 4 East Zion day, 45 min via the tunnel: horseback at East Zion Adventures (Pine Knoll 2 h or Cowboy Cookout 3 h), then an intro canyoneering trip (Rock Drop, dry rappels). Checkerboard Mesa is beside the road. Pick up Narrows dry suits at Zion Adventures in Springdale on the way back (open to 6 pm). Apply for the Dec 5 lottery. Zion Lodge
Sat Dec 5 Big hike. Angels Landing if drawn: park at the Grotto at first light, gloves and microspikes for the chains. If not drawn: Scout Lookout and the West Rim beyond, no permit. Or the Narrows bottom-up in dry suits from Temple of Sinawava. Emerald Pools-Kayenta loop in the afternoon. Apply for the Dec 6 lottery. Zion Lodge
Sun Dec 6 Whichever of Angels Landing or the Narrows is undone. Riverside Walk, Pa'rus at sunset. Watchman Trail is closed. Alternative: Wire Pass slot canyon near Kanab, 1 h away, $6 pp, sedan OK when dry. Zion Lodge
Mon Dec 7 Check out. Drive to Torrey. Dry: UT-12 over Boulder Mountain via Escalante and Boulder, 4 h 50, one of the great drives. Any recent storm: US-89 N, I-70 E, UT-24 via Loa, 4 h 40. Arrive by 4:30, sunset 5:05. Dinner at the Red Sands eatery; carry a backup. Red Sands Hotel, Torrey.
Tue Dec 8 Capitol Reef, visitor center opens 9. Hickman Bridge (1.8 mi), Cassidy Arch (3.4 mi, icy slickrock, microspikes), Grand Wash. Capitol Gorge road is closed as of Nov 10. Leave Fruita by 1 pm. UT-24 W, I-70 E, US-50, I-15 N to SLC, 4 h 15, arriving about 5:30. Return the car that evening (no penalty) and shuttle to the hotel, or keep it until morning. SLC airport hotel (Hilton Garden Inn, SpringHill Suites, or Courtyard).
Wed Dec 9 Car back by 10 if not returned the night before. Flight 11:25.

Weather swaps

  • Dec 2 UT-20 closed: low route via I-70 (above). Sunset at Bryce is lost; sunrise Dec 3 remains.
  • Zion days 4-6 all swap with each other. Dec 4 is the anchor (operator bookings); the Narrows closes above 150 cfs or on a flash-flood warning; Angels Landing chains close in ice.
  • Dec 7 Boulder Mountain: decide at breakfast from udottraffic.utah.gov.
  • Dec 8 storm on US-50 or I-15: leave Capitol Reef earlier, skip Grand Wash.
