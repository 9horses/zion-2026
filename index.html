Itinerary

Plan as of 2026-11-21. All times Mountain. Daylight about 9 h 40 m; sunset 5:05-5:14 pm everywhere on this loop.

Fixed points

Wed Dec 2 UA1910 lands SLC 10:01 am. Hertz Volvo XC60 (AWD) .
Thu Dec 3 Zion Lodge check-in (4 pm). Red Rock Grill dinner 5 pm (booked).
Dec 3-7 Zion Lodge, 4 nights, check-out Mon Dec 7 (11 am, or 1 pm with the winter promo).
Tue Dec 8 Courtyard by Marriott Salt Lake City Downtown (booked).
Wed Dec 9 Hertz return SLC by 10:00 am. UA1527 departs 11:25 am.

Day by day

Wed Dec 2 - SLC to Bryce

  • Land 10:01. At the Hertz counter: decline all protection, add Cecilia as driver, check the tires are M+S or 3PMSF.
  • Drive I-15 S, UT-20, US-89 S, UT-12 E to Bryce, 4 h 15. Lunch stop at Fillmore or Beaver.
  • If UT-20 is closed: I-15 to I-70, I-70 E to US-89, south to Panguitch, 4 h 45.
  • Buy the America the Beautiful pass at the Bryce gate ($80, covers all three parks). Arrive about 3:45.
  • Sunset Point for the 5:10 sunset (Bryce map).
  • Dinner Cowboy's Buffet.
  • Night: Ruby's Inn, Bryce Canyon City.

Thu Dec 3 - Bryce to Zion

  • Sunrise 7:27 at Sunrise Point. The hoodoos face east; this is the light.
  • Queen's Garden + Navajo Loop via Two Bridges, about 3 mi, microspikes. Then Inspiration and Bryce Points.
  • Apply for the Dec 4 Angels Landing lottery before leaving Bryce (by 3 pm, signal is patchy after).
  • Leave 12:30. UT-12, US-89 S, UT-9 W through the tunnel, 2 h 15.
  • Canyon Overlook Trail (1 mi) sits just east of the tunnel; do it on the way in if there is light.
  • Check in 4 pm. Dinner 5 pm, Red Rock Grill (booked).
  • Night: Zion Lodge.

Fri Dec 4 - East Zion

  • 45 min via the tunnel. Checkerboard Mesa is beside the road.
  • Horseback at East Zion Adventures: Pine Knoll 2 h or Cowboy Cookout 3 h.
  • Then an intro canyoneering trip (Rock Drop, dry rappels).
  • Pick up Narrows dry suits at Zion Adventures in Springdale on the way back (open to 6 pm).
  • Apply for the Dec 5 lottery.
  • Night: Zion Lodge.

Sat Dec 5 - Big hike

  • Angels Landing if drawn: park at the Grotto at first light, gloves and microspikes for the chains.
  • If not drawn: Scout Lookout and the West Rim beyond, no permit needed.
  • Or the Narrows bottom-up in dry suits from Temple of Sinawava.
  • Emerald Pools-Kayenta loop in the afternoon.
  • Apply for the Dec 6 lottery.
  • Night: Zion Lodge.

Sun Dec 6 - The other one

  • Whichever of Angels Landing or the Narrows is undone.
  • Riverside Walk, Pa'rus at sunset. Watchman Trail is closed.
  • Alternative: Wire Pass slot canyon near Kanab, 1 h away, $6 pp, sedan OK when dry.
  • Night: Zion Lodge.

Mon Dec 7 - Zion to Torrey

  • Check out (11 am, or 1 pm with the winter promo).
  • Dry roads: UT-12 over Boulder Mountain via Escalante and Boulder, 4 h 50, one of the great drives.
  • Any recent storm: US-89 N, I-70 E, UT-24 via Loa, 4 h 40.
  • Arrive by 4:30, sunset 5:05.
  • Dinner at the Red Sands eatery; carry a backup.
  • Night: Red Sands Hotel, Torrey.

Tue Dec 8 - Capitol Reef to Salt Lake City

  • Visitor center opens 9. Hickman Bridge (1.8 mi), Cassidy Arch (3.4 mi, icy slickrock, microspikes), Grand Wash.
  • Capitol Gorge road is closed as of Nov 10.
  • Leave Fruita by 1 pm. UT-24 W, I-70 E, US-50, I-15 N to downtown SLC, 4 h 15, arriving about 5:30.
  • Keep the car overnight; hotel parking is a daily fee.
  • Evening downtown: Temple Square is a 10 min walk and the Christmas lights run through the holidays. Dinner nearby.
  • Night: Courtyard by Marriott Salt Lake City Downtown (booked).

Wed Dec 9 - Fly home

  • Airport is 15 min from downtown. Leave the hotel by 9:15, car back by 10:00.
  • Flight 11:25.

Weather swaps

  • Dec 2 UT-20 closed: low route via I-70 (above). Sunset at Bryce is lost; sunrise Dec 3 remains.
  • Zion days 4-6 all swap with each other. Dec 4 is the anchor (operator bookings); the Narrows closes above 150 cfs or on a flash-flood warning; Angels Landing chains close in ice.
  • Dec 7 Boulder Mountain: decide at breakfast from udottraffic.utah.gov.
  • Dec 8 storm on US-50 or I-15: leave Capitol Reef earlier, skip Grand Wash.
