Itinerary

Day by day

Times are targets, not bookings, except where marked. Sunrise about 7:30, sunset 5:05 to 5:14 all week.

Wed Dec 2 - SLC to Bryce

  • 10:01 Land. Wait for the checked bag (poles). Hertz Gold: skip the counter, go to the board and the car. Protection is declined (checked in the app). Cecilia drives as an Authorized Operator under Hertz's US terms (spouse of a Gold member, no fee, no listing needed); she should carry her licence. Glance at the tire sidewalls while loading bags. M+S or 3PMSF means the car is legal on a road under a storm traction restriction; no mark just means Boulder Mountain is out on a storm day. Not worth a car swap.
  • 10:45 to 11:00 Rolling. Eat the packed late breakfast in the car; rest areas on I-15 have vending only and the gas stops (Nephi, Scipio Flying J at exit 188, Fillmore) are the only food before Beaver. Set destination to Bryce Canyon Visitor Center. I-15 S, UT-20, US-89 S, UT-12 E. 4 h 15 of driving.
  • 11:15 Fallback if UT-20 is closed: I-15 to I-70, I-70 E to US-89, south to Panguitch. 4 h 45 total, eat in the car, sunset doubtful.
  • 1:45 to 2:00 Lunch at Arshel's Cafe in Beaver (exit 112, Wed 11 to 7, confirm 435-438-2977). Sit down if the clock allows, 45 min; to go if not. A break here is the point as much as the food. Fallback is the fast-food cluster at the same exit.
  • 3:45 to 4:00 Bryce gate. Buy the America the Beautiful pass ($80, covers all three parks).
  • 4:30 to 5:15 Sunset Point for the 5:10 sunset (Bryce map).
  • 5:30 Ruby's Inn, check in, dinner at Cowboy's Buffet.
  • Night: Ruby's Inn, Bryce Canyon City.

Thu Dec 3 - Bryce to Zion

  • 7:00 Leave Ruby's for Sunrise Point. 7:27 sunrise. The hoodoos face east; this is the light.
  • 8:00 to 10:30 Queen's Garden + Navajo Loop via Two Bridges, about 3 mi, microspikes.
  • 10:30 to 11:45 Inspiration Point, then Bryce Point.
  • Before 12:30 Apply for the Dec 4 Angels Landing lottery (deadline 3 pm; do it on Ruby's wifi, signal is patchy after Bryce Canyon City).
  • 12:00 Lunch to go from Canyon Diner at Ruby's (opens 11:30, order ahead online, winter menu).
  • 12:30 Leave. Set destination to Zion Lodge. UT-12, US-89 S, UT-9 W, 2 h 15.
  • 2:45 to 3:45 Canyon Overlook Trail (1 mi), just east of the tunnel, while there is light.
  • 4:00 Check in at Zion Lodge.
  • 5:00 Dinner, Red Rock Grill (booked).
  • Night: Zion Lodge.

Fri Dec 4 - East Zion

  • Breakfast Apply for the Dec 5 lottery on lodge wifi (deadline 3 pm).
  • 8:00 Leave. Set destination to East Zion Adventures. 45 min via the tunnel; Checkerboard Mesa is beside the road.
  • Morning Horseback at East Zion Adventures: Pine Knoll 2 h or Cowboy Cookout 3 h. Start time set when booked.
  • Lunch Cordwood at Zion Mountain Ranch, 4 mi east of the park entrance (11 to 4:30, open all winter), or its The Get for grab-and-go. Thunderbird at Mt Carmel Junction is the fallback.
  • Afternoon Intro canyoneering trip (Rock Drop, dry rappels), about 3 h. Start time set when booked.
  • By 5:30 Pick up Narrows dry suits at Zion Adventures in Springdale (closes 6 pm).
  • Night: Zion Lodge.

Sat Dec 5 - Big hike

  • Breakfast Apply for the Dec 6 lottery (deadline 3 pm).
  • 7:15 If drawn: park at the Grotto at first light. Angels Landing 4 to 5 h, gloves and microspikes for the chains.
  • 7:15 If not drawn: same trailhead, Scout Lookout and the West Rim beyond, no permit, 3 to 4 h.
  • 8:30 Narrows option instead: start at Temple of Sinawava once light reaches the canyon floor, turn around by noon, out by 2:30.
  • 12:30 Lunch at Red Rock Grill (11:30 to 4, takeout at the host stand). Castle Dome Cafe is closed for winter. For a sit-down in Springdale: Oscar's Cafe (8 to 8, confirm 435-772-3232).
  • 1:30 to 4:00 Emerald Pools-Kayenta loop, from the Zion Lodge footbridge.
  • Night: Zion Lodge.

Sun Dec 6 - The other one

  • Morning Whichever of Angels Landing or the Narrows is undone, same start times as Dec 5.
  • Lunch Red Rock Grill takeout, or a trail lunch from the Springdale run on Dec 4.
  • 3:00 Riverside Walk from Temple of Sinawava, then Pa'rus from Canyon Junction for the 5:10 sunset.
  • Alternative day 7:30 leave for Wire Pass Trailhead slot canyon near Kanab, 1 h each way, 3 to 4 h in the canyon, $6 pp, sedan OK when dry. Back by 3:30.
  • Watchman Trail is closed.
  • Night: Zion Lodge.

Mon Dec 7 - Zion to Torrey

  • 8:00 Breakfast. Decide the route from udottraffic.utah.gov.
  • 9:30 Leave (check-out is 11, but the drive needs the daylight). Pack the night before.
  • 9:30 Set destination to Red Sands Hotel. Dry roads, or snowy with M+S tires and no closure: UT-12 over Boulder Mountain via Escalante and Boulder, 4 h 50, one of the great drives.
  • 9:30 Any recent storm: US-89 N, I-70 E, UT-24 via Loa, 4 h 40.
  • 1:00 Lunch. Escalante is nearly shut in December: Escalante Outfitters closed Nov 17, Hell's Backbone Grill in Boulder closed Nov 29, Nemo's looks gone. Circle D Eatery (Mon 7 to 9:30 on paper, call 435-826-4125) is the only candidate. Carry a packed lunch from Red Rock Grill takeout at breakfast either way; on the I-70 route there is nothing until Loa.
  • 3:00 to 4:30 Arrive Torrey, before the 5:05 sunset.
  • 6:00 Dinner at the Red Sands eatery; carry a backup.
  • Night: Red Sands Hotel, Torrey.

Tue Dec 8 - Capitol Reef to Salt Lake City

  • 8:00 Leave Torrey. Set destination to Hickman Bridge Trailhead, 15 min.
  • 8:15 to 9:30 Hickman Bridge (1.8 mi).
  • 9:45 to 12:15 Cassidy Arch (3.4 mi, icy slickrock, microspikes) from the Grand Wash trailhead, then a short walk into Grand Wash. Capitol Gorge road is closed as of Nov 10.
  • 12:30 Visitor center for a stamp and the restroom.
  • 1:00 Leave Fruita. Set destination to Courtyard SLC Downtown. UT-24 W, I-70 E, US-50, I-15 N, 4 h 15.
  • 2:15 Lunch at Mom's Cafe in Salina, right off the route (Tue 7 to 7). Nothing in the park; Gifford House is closed for winter.
  • 5:30 Courtyard SLC Downtown. Keep the car overnight; hotel parking is a daily fee.
  • Evening Temple Square is a 10 min walk; the Christmas lights run through the holidays. Dinner nearby.
  • Night: Courtyard by Marriott Salt Lake City Downtown (booked).

Wed Dec 9 - Fly home

  • 9:15 Leave the hotel. Set destination to SLC rental car return, 15 min.
  • 10:00 Car back at Hertz.
  • 11:25 Flight.

Weather swaps

  • Dec 2 UT-20 closed: low route via I-70 (above). Sunset at Bryce is lost; sunrise Dec 3 remains.
  • Zion days 4-6 all swap with each other. Dec 4 is the anchor (operator bookings); the Narrows closes above 150 cfs or on a flash-flood warning; Angels Landing chains close in ice.
  • Dec 7 Boulder Mountain: decide at breakfast from udottraffic.utah.gov.
  • Dec 8 storm on US-50 or I-15: leave Capitol Reef earlier, skip Grand Wash.
